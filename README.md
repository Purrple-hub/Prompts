## 🚀 How to Use

1. **Pick a prompt** – browse the `.md` files and choose the one that fits your task.
2. **Copy the content** – paste it into your favourite AI interface (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, etc.).
3. **Fill in the placeholders** – most prompts include `[bracketed]` sections that you should replace with your actual context.
4. **Iterate** – don’t be afraid to tweak the prompt to better suit your specific problem.

## 🛠️ Customisation Tips

- **Adjust the tone** – add or remove adjectives to match your preferred level of formality.
- **Add constraints** – e.g., “use only standard library”, “avoid recursion”, or “target Python 3.11”.
- **Combine prompts** – sometimes chaining two prompts yields even better results.
